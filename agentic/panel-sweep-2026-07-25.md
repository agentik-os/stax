Every line carries the evidence its agent actually collected. Re-verify before
acting: an unrefuted claim is a lead, not a fact.

### account · contact · opportunity · activity (the CRM record family)

- **[blocker]** The dense-bar "type glyph" is the same empty circle for every panel type in the app
  - evidence: App.tsx:1632 passes a RESOURCE key into a SPACE-id switch: `<SpaceIcon id={p.target.resourceKey} />`. SpaceIcon (App.tsx:217-236) only cases on space ids ("crm", "overview", "pf-console"…), so "acc:acme", "con:jo", "opp:refonte", "act:call1" and even "space:crm" all fall to App.tsx:234 `default: <circle cx="12" cy="12" r="9"/>`. Runtime (CRM-02/CRM-04, light+dark, after reload): glyph innerHTML is byte-identical `<circle cx="12" cy="12" r="9"></circle>` on all 5 chain panels AND on report/doc/pfkeys/pfhealth/anblotter/antreasury. Screenshot SH-bars-light.png shows three bars side by side with the identical hollow circle. DESIGN-SPEC §8 dense-bar: "The bar carries type glyph + title + LIVE numbers".
  - fix: Stop calling SpaceIcon with a resourceKey. Add a `TypeIcon({panelType})` keyed on the registry type (building for account, person for contact, target/trend for opportunity, calendar-check for activity, per §5: viewBox 24, strokeWidth 1.6-1.7, currentColor, 14px in the bar), fall back to SpaceIcon only for space roots. One glyph per registry entry, so a new panel type declares its mark the way it declares its size.
- **[blocker]** The record's own data is invisible under the default head layout: subtitle folds into a 16px ⓘ tooltip
  - evidence: In the CRM the subtitle IS the record (domain.ts:550 opp:refonte subtitle "€18,000 · proposal sent"; :558 act:call1 "Jul 12 · 30 min"; :538 acc:acme "Manufacturing · Lyon"; :544 con:jo "Operations director"). The default head layout sets `bodySub: false` (headLayouts.ts:112-115 denseBar) and the only survivor is a title attribute on a 16px button (App.tsx:1674-1676). Runtime (CRM-07) on the deep link #/crm/opportunity~opp:refonte: `needleAnywhereOnStage:false` for "€18,000" — the amount and stage appear nowhere on the stage or in the panel; the body's first block is instead framework meta-commentary ("opportunity · M 480 · depth 3…"). Same for "Jul 12" on the activity and "Operations director" on the contact.
  - fix: A record is not a document: give this family real field groups. Per DESIGN-SPEC §6 ("Form/inspector card → the title IS the field … every remaining group is a flat .section, form cards are FORBIDDEN"), split subtitle into typed fields (amount, stage, owner, close date / industry, city / role, email / date, duration, channel) and render them as .section blocks in the body. Until then, at minimum promote the record line into .bar-meta instead of "n items".
- **[major]** bar-meta prints the generic "2 items" on account, contact and opportunity alike, while the domain already authors the right noun
  - evidence: App.tsx:1671 `bits.push(DOMAIN[key].children.length + " items")`.   - fix: Fall back to `DOMAIN[key].meta` before the generic count in the barMeta builder (App.tsx:1665-1672), or derive the noun from the child's panelType (contact→contacts, opportunity→opportunities, activity→activities). Live numbers must stay derived — the noun can be too.
- **[major]** Money renders in the sans body font, non-tabular — the family's only numbers break the type law
  - evidence: styles.css:219 `.drill .ss { font-size: 12px; color: var(--muted-foreground); }` — no font-family, no font-variant-numeric. - **[major]** The drill list — the entire body of account/contact/opportunity — ignores the user's Body-size preference
  - evidence: styles.css:218 `.drill .tt { font-size: 13px }`, :219 `.drill .ss { font-size: 12px }`, :227 `.leaf-note { … font-size: 13px … }` are hardcoded px.
