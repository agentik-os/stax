# U-043: the front door is a panel, and denial is a designed state

TARGET: {{TARGET}}
REFERENCE: `stax-migrate shapes entry`, `stax-migrate shapes refusal`

## DETECT

- Grep the app for its sign in, invitation and workspace-picker surfaces. If
  they are built from different primitives than the panels behind them, the
  product has two design systems and the seam is at its most sensitive screen.
- Look for a control that is rendered DISABLED with a tooltip naming the
  permission it needs. That tooltip is a capability oracle.
- Look for a count rendered over a filtered set. If the number includes rows the
  reader cannot open, the count leaks their existence.
- Read the sign in error copy. "No account with that address" is an account
  oracle and it is the most common leak in the flow.

## APPLY

1. AN ENTRY IS A PANEL, at the registry M width, with the same bar, body padding
   and foot as everything behind it. Several entrances differ ONLY in the
   eyebrow, the copy, the sign in method and the refusal set.
2. A LANDING PAGE ROUTES AND BRANDS. It never authorises. Capabilities are the
   only gate; visibility configuration can only hide. One identity instance for
   every entrance, or one human holds two subjects and the identity argument dies.
3. A DENIED ELEMENT RENDERS ITS EMPTY EXPRESSION, OR NOTHING. Never hidden but
   present, never greyed with a tooltip, never masked, never counted.
4. EVERY REFUSAL SAYS WHAT THE PERSON DOES NEXT, and names neither the missing
   membership nor the missing capability: that answer describes your business.
5. There is no degraded read-only state for a suspended account. Suspended is
   not a smaller set of grants, it is none.

## VERIFY

- Switch entry and assert the copy and the method change while the width, the
  bar height and the body padding do NOT.
- Drive every refusal, read the sentence each one shows, and fail on an
  existence disclosure, a capability name, or a refusal with no next step.
- Assert a reference surface carries no primary action.
