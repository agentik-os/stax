# U-037: the head layouts — a panel's identity becomes a data plan

TARGET: {{TARGET}}
REFERENCE: frameword/apps/crm-specimen/src/headLayouts.ts + the Panel render

## DETECT
- Every panel prints a serif title plus a description line in its BODY. Measure
  it on the real app: bar (44) + head (75 to 122) + foot (44) spends 28% of a
  panel's height on a 13in laptop, 32% on 1280x700.
- Drill from a row and compare: the child panel's title AND description repeat
  the row that opened it, word for word, while that row stays on screen. In a
  Miller-column UI this is structural, not accidental. Measure the rate: it is
  usually 100%.
- Identity is stated up to FOUR times: crumbbar, bar eyebrow, the visible parent
  row, the body title.
- The head is hardcoded in the panel render: there is no way to change the
  treatment without forking the component.

## APPLY
1. THE REGISTRY: `headLayouts.ts` exports a `HeadLayout` union, a `HEAD_LAYOUTS`
   catalog (id, proposal, label, blurb, reclaims) and ONE pure function
   `headPlan(layout, ctx) -> HeadPlan`. The Panel renders from the plan and
   decides nothing itself; a new treatment is a row in the table.
   Context: isRoot, isRef, focused, echoesParent, scrolled, seen, compact,
   hasSubtitle. Plan: barEyebrow, barGlyph, barTitle, barMeta, bodyTitle,
   bodySub, info, spine, titleScale.
2. THE TEN TREATMENTS: dense-bar (DEFAULT), echo, no-subtitle, bar-title,
   focus-only, scroll-collapse, first-run, spine, density, editorial. See the
   spec table for what each does and what it gives back.
3. THE BINDING LAWS: a panel ALWAYS states its identity somewhere; the foot
   stays the only action zone; the bar folds by priority under 640px (numbers
   first, eyebrow next, title never); a bar title is editable exactly where the
   node is the user's own (double-click, inline, Escape cancels); the live
   numbers are DERIVED (row count, first KPI, child count), never authored.
4. THE SETTINGS SURFACE: the appearance panel lists the catalog with each
   treatment's blurb and reclaim, marks the app default, and reveals the
   cozy/compact/dense rhythm when the `density` treatment is picked. The choice
   is device-local, like theme and zoom.

## VERIFY
- With the default, a doc panel shows `.bar-title` and NO `.panel-title` or
  `.panel-sub`; bar + foot stay under 20% of the panel height.
- For all ten treatments, the leaf panel matches
  `.bar-title, .panel-title, .panel-spine, .eyebrow` and the document never
  scrolls sideways.
- scroll-collapse: the body title exists at rest and is gone past 220px of
  scroll, with the bar title taking over. focus-only: the leaf folds when the
  parent bar is clicked. first-run: the head shows on visit 1 and is gone after
  a reload. echo: a root keeps its title, a drill does not.
- spine: the rail names the panel and the first row clears the rail's right edge.
- Renaming: double-click a data table's bar title, type, Enter — the parent row
  shows the new name (the store was written, not just the label).
